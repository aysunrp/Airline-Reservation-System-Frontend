Cabin Experience

Three ways to fly with AEROVA

Economy, Comfort, and Business are composed as distinct cabins — each with its own seat, service, and pace. Choose a class to see exactly what is included.

01

Economy Class

A calm, well-equipped cabin for everyday and long-haul travel.

02

Comfort Class

More room, priority care, and a quieter place to settle.

03

Business Class

Privacy, a lie-flat bed, and service arranged around your time.

Economy Class

A considered cabin for every journey

Economy is designed for ease: a supportive seat, a personal screen, and service that stays attentive without crowding the cabin.

  • Seat comfort

    Ergonomic seats with an adjustable headrest and a measured recline for medium and long-haul flights.

  • Seat configuration

    3-3-3 on wide-body aircraft and 3-3 on narrow-body, with a seatback screen at every place.

  • Entertainment

    Films, series, and music on the seatback system, plus a moving map for the route ahead.

  • Wi-Fi and connectivity

    Onboard Wi-Fi on equipped aircraft, with messaging included on selected routes.

  • Meals and beverages

    A hot meal or snack according to flight length, with complimentary soft drinks, tea, and coffee.

  • Cabin baggage

    One cabin bag up to 8 kg, plus a personal item that fits beneath the seat in front of you.

  • Checked baggage

    One checked bag up to 23 kg on international itineraries.

  • USB and power

    USB-A and USB-C charging at every seat. AC power is fitted on selected long-haul aircraft.

  • Passenger service

    Cabin crew trained to be present when you need them, with a call button at every seat.

  • Included benefits

    Complimentary water, seat selection from 24 hours before departure, and AEROVA entertainment for the full flight.

Comfort Class

More space, with priority from the start

Comfort sits between Economy and Business: extra legroom, a quieter cabin zone, and services that move you through the airport with less waiting.

  • Extra legroom

    Greater seat pitch than Economy, so you can stretch and settle for the length of the flight.

  • More spacious seating

    Wider seats with deeper cushioning and a more generous recline than the main cabin.

  • Enhanced comfort

    A quieter cabin section, a larger pillow, and an amenity kit on long-haul flights.

  • Priority services

    Priority check-in, priority boarding, and earlier delivery of your checked bag on arrival.

  • Meals and beverages

    An upgraded menu with a wider choice, plus complimentary drinks including selected wines.

  • Entertainment

    A larger high-definition screen and noise-reducing headphones.

  • Wi-Fi and connectivity

    Complimentary messaging and a Wi-Fi allowance on equipped aircraft.

  • Baggage benefits

    Two cabin bags and one checked bag up to 30 kg.

  • USB and power

    USB and AC power at every Comfort seat, so devices stay ready for the flight.

  • Included benefits

    Dedicated overhead space in the Comfort cabin, priority boarding, and a welcome drink after take-off.

Business Class

A private cabin, arranged around you

Business is AEROVA’s most composed way to fly: a spacious seat that becomes a bed, dining served to your schedule, and priority from the kerb to the gate.

  • Premium spacious seating

    A private, suite-style seat with direct aisle access on wide-body aircraft.

  • Lie-flat rest

    A fully flat bed on long-haul flights, with a mattress topper, duvet, and pillow.

  • Priority check-in

    Dedicated Business desks, so you move from arrival at the airport to your boarding pass with less waiting.

  • Priority boarding

    Board before the main cabin and settle into your seat while the aircraft is still quiet.

  • Lounge access

    Access to AEROVA and partner lounges before departure, with quiet seating and a full refreshment service.

  • Premium dining

    A multi-course menu with seasonal dishes, served when you are ready rather than on a fixed round.

  • Premium entertainment

    A large screen, studio-quality headphones, and an extended film and music library.

  • Wi-Fi and connectivity

    Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi for the flight on equipped aircraft.

  • Increased baggage allowance

    Two cabin bags and two checked bags of up to 32 kg each.

  • Premium cabin service

    A dedicated crew, a welcome amenity, and turndown service when you are ready to rest.

  • Included benefits

    Lounge access, priority at check-in, security, and boarding, plus a lie-flat bed on long-haul flights.

Beyond the seat

The journey, from the airport to arrival

  1. 01

    On the ground

    Comfort and Business guests use priority check-in. Where the airport allows it, Fast Track shortens the path to the gate.

  2. 02

    In the cabin

    Cabin lighting follows the flight. The crew is trained to be present when needed, then leave you to the journey.

  3. 03

    On arrival

    Comfort and Business baggage is delivered ahead of the main hold, so the journey ends with the same composure it began.
