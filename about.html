Our Story

Crafted for Modern Travel

AEROVA began with a simple ambition: to make premium air travel feel effortless. From our home in Baku, we connect cities and coastlines with a quieter kind of luxury — one defined by service, design, and trust.

Today, travelers choose AEROVA for journeys that are as thoughtfully prepared as the destinations they seek.
